How it works

The app does four things. Only what is in the app today is written here.

1. One tap to save

Six buttons on the home screen — formula, breastfeeding, diaper, sleep, solids, temperature. One tap saves. Tapped by mistake? Undo within five seconds. Press and hold to add details like amount or site.

At night, a night screen: a big clock and two big buttons — asleep, awake. Dark, so it is easier on the eyes.

2. Kept as written

A corrected entry is marked "corrected", and who changed it, and when, stays in its history. The original is never deleted. If you did not measure the leftover, it stays blank — not zero. Hours with nothing recorded say "no records".

3. The whole family writes

Invite family with one code. It works once, expires after 72 hours, and can be revoked any time. Three roles — write together, add only, view. Every entry shows who wrote it.

4. To the doctor, as is

The report lists the last 7 days — temperature, medicine, symptoms, new foods, feeding — as facts. No interpretation, no normal ranges. Share it as text and show it at the clinic. It always ends with: "Abu records. Not a medical diagnosis."
