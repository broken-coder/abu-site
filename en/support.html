Support

Contact: support@abu.care

Can I use it without an account?
Yes. Your baby's name is all it takes. Sign in with Apple is only needed to write with family or sync between devices.
Whose records are they?
Yours. Write to the address above and we return everything, or delete it if you ask.
How do I show it to the doctor?
From the Report tab, share the last 7 days as text. Facts only, no interpretation.
How do I invite family?
Make an invite code in the Family tab and send it. It works once and expires after 72 hours. You can revoke it any time.
Does Abu tell me how my baby is doing?
No. Abu only records. Judging is for you and your doctor.
